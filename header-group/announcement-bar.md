# Announcement Bar

The **Announcement Bar Section** allows you to display important messages, promotions, or updates at the top of your store. It enhances customer engagement and improves visibility for key information.

{% hint style="success" %}
1. **Go to** Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes.
2. **Click** **Customize** on your active theme.
3. In the theme editor, **click** **Add Section > Announcement Bar**.
{% endhint %}

### Settings & Customization

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Announcement-bar (1).jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Announcement bar**

* **Make Section Full Width** : Enable this option to extend the announcement bar across the entire screen width.
* **Color scheme:** You can customize the section’s appearance by changing the **text color, background color**, and more using **preset color** options.
* **Hide on Mobile** : Enable/disable the announcement bar on mobile devices as needed
* **Enable Edge Fade**: Adds a subtle fade effect on the edges of the bar for a polished visual transition.

**Currency selector**

* **Currency Selector**: Enable a dropdown to let users choose their preferred currency.\
  \&#xNAN;_Note: Add countries/regions in your_ [_**Market settings**_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/managing)_**.**_

**Language selector**

* **Language Selector**: Enable a dropdown to allow customers to choose their preferred language.\
  \&#xNAN;_Note: Languages must be configured in your_ [_**Language settings**_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/localization-and-translation)_**.**_

**Social media icons**

* **Enable Social Icons** : Enable to display social media icons linked to your store’s accounts (ensure accounts are added in [**Theme Settings**](../theme-settings/social-icon.md) ).

**Animation Settings**

* **Direction**: Choose the scrolling direction of the announcement text – **Left to Right** or **Right to Left**.
* **Speed**: Set the scroll speed. _(Default: 80 – lower values scroll faster)_
* **Pause on Hover**: Enable this option to pause the scrolling animation when a user hovers over the announcement bar for easier reading.

**Theme Settings & Customization**

* Adjust additional styles in **Theme Settings**.
* Use **Custom CSS** for further design modifications.

### Add an Announcement

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Announcement-bar-02 (1).jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="success" %}
Go to **Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Customize > click Announcement Bar > Add Announcement.**
{% endhint %}

#### **Add Announcement**

* **Text**: Add the message to display in the announcement bar.\
  \&#xNAN;_Example: “Subscribe & get 5% off on all paintings”_
* **Link URL**: Paste a link or search for a page within your store.\
  Clicking the announcement text will redirect users to this URL.
