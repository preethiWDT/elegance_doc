Announcement Bar

Announcement Bar

The Announcement Bar Section allows you to display important messages, promotions, or updates at the top of your store. It enhances customer engagement and improves visibility for key information.

TIP:

  1. Go to Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes.
  2. Click Customize on your active theme.
  3. In the theme editor, click Add Section > Announcement Bar.

Settings & Customization

Announcement bar

  • Make Section Full Width : Enable this option to extend the announcement bar across the entire screen width.
  • Color scheme: You can customize the section’s appearance by changing the text color, background color, and more using preset color options.
  • Hide on Mobile : Enable/disable the announcement bar on mobile devices as needed
  • Enable Edge Fade: Adds a subtle fade effect on the edges of the bar for a polished visual transition.

Currency selector

  • Currency Selector: Enable a dropdown to let users choose their preferred currency.\ \\&#xNAN;Note: Add countries/regions in your Market settings.

Language selector

  • Language Selector: Enable a dropdown to allow customers to choose their preferred language.\ \\&#xNAN;Note: Languages must be configured in your Language settings.

Social media icons

  • Enable Social Icons : Enable to display social media icons linked to your store’s accounts (ensure accounts are added in Theme Settings ).

Animation Settings

  • Direction: Choose the scrolling direction of the announcement text – Left to Right or Right to Left.
  • Speed: Set the scroll speed. (Default: 80 – lower values scroll faster)
  • Pause on Hover: Enable this option to pause the scrolling animation when a user hovers over the announcement bar for easier reading.

Theme Settings & Customization

  • Adjust additional styles in Theme Settings.
  • Use Custom CSS for further design modifications.

Add an Announcement

TIP: Go to Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Customize > click Announcement Bar > Add Announcement.

Add Announcement

  • Text: Add the message to display in the announcement bar.\ \\&#xNAN;Example: “Subscribe & get 5% off on all paintings”
  • Link URL: Paste a link or search for a page within your store.\ Clicking the announcement text will redirect users to this URL.
